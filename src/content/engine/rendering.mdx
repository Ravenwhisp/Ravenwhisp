Rendering development includes work on the lighting pipeline and real-time shadows. The shadow mapping implementation renders depth from a directional light, then uses that shadow data during the scene’s lighting pass.

## Shadow mapping

The shadow map is first used as a depth target and later sampled by the lighting shader. The implementation establishes the project’s initial real-time shadowing workflow, with later posts covering follow-up improvements.
